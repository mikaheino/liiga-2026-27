How this is made
No crystal ball — just goals, ages and a lot of maths
1
Start with players, not teamsEvery team is rebuilt from the players actually on its roster today. Transfers are baked in from day one.
2
Count the goals they really scoredFive seasons of scoring history for each player, with the most recent season counting the most.
3
Adjust for age and leagueA 20-year-old and a 38-year-old are not the same bet. Neither is a goal in the AHL and a goal in Liiga.
4
Add the goaliesSave percentage decides how many goals a team lets in — often the difference between a playoff spot and a golden helmet.
5
Play the season 10,000 timesEvery one of the 60 games is simulated over and over. The table shows the average result, not a single guess.
Two models vote on every game — a goal-scoring model (40%) and a team-strength rating (60%) — then a crowd of 20% fan predictions is mixed in. Tested on past seasons, it lands within about 12.7 points of a team's real total.
