Liiga 2026–27 · Under the hood
The model,
and what's next
The match model Goals are drawn from a Poisson model, λ = lgAvg × off × def × home_ice, with a Dixon-Coles correction inflating the low-score diagonal so the simulated OT rate matches Liiga's observed 23%. That is blended 40/60 with a margin-of-victory Elo rating (k=16).
Validation Leakage-free backtest over 2023–26, each season rated on prior seasons only: points MAE 12.65, game log-loss 0.672 against a 0.686 base rate, standings Spearman ρ 0.48. Tuned on log-loss and MAE — ρ is far too noisy at n=4 to optimise against.
Next: Snowflake ML jobs Porting the pipeline to Snowflake ML jobs on a nightly schedule — ingesting box scores, refitting Elo on current results, and re-simulating only unplayed fixtures while banking points already earned. The pre-season prior decays as real games accumulate.

I will publish the error as it moves — including where it was wrong.
