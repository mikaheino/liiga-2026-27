Age drags scoring down — and not gently. Real Liiga data: players score at just two-thirds of their rate the very next season once they hit 35, and less past 38. Kousa, 39, is deep in that zone: his 0.045 gets cut to 0.03.

Goaltending drives defence. Not last year's goals-against (too dependent on opponents) — each goalie's own save%. Pelicans' Patrik Bartosak projects a .911 save% vs the league's .908 average: worth about 0.08 fewer goals against per start.
6
Elo tracks who's actually winning — and by how much. Every team starts at 1500. Wins transfer points from loser to winner, scaled by surprise and by goal margin.

8
Overtime reality check. Pure math predicts ~16% of games go to OT; the real Liiga rate is 23% — Pelicans' games included. The model inflates tie odds to match, and treats OT as a near coin flip (52% to the favourite) — worth 2 points instead of 3.
9
40 fans add their two cents. Before puck drop, 40 Jatkoaika.com forum members predicted the final table — they had Pelicans 14th. The model has them 12th. Their average rank, converted to points, gets blended in at 20% weight — phased out as real games replace it.
10
The season is simulated 10 000 times. Every remaining game plays out at its modeled odds, 10 000 times over. That's how Pelicans land at 92 points, 12th place — with a realistic range of 80–114, not one fixed number.
Model: Bottom-up player goal production (5-season recency-weighted history, age curve) → Poisson match model with goaltending-driven defense and an overtime-rate calibration (Liiga's observed 23% OT/SO share). Blended with margin-of-victory Elo (slow k, trained on 2022–2026 results). Crowd signal: mean predicted rank from 40 Jatkoaika.com forum members, converted to expected-points scale and weighted at 20%.
Playoffs: Top 6 advance directly to the quarterfinals. Ranks 7–10 play a best-of-three qualification round for the last two spots — shown as its own probability column above (the round itself is not simulated).
Relegation: new for 2026-27 — the bottom three drop to the B series. The model does not treat those games differently; the red line only marks where the table splits.
